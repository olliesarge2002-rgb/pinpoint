Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

The short version. Pinpoint stores the places, photos and profile details you add so you and the people who follow you can see them. We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

Who we are

Pinpoint is run by Ollie Sargeant, an individual based in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

DataWhy
Account: email address and password (stored in hashed form by our hosting provider)To create your account and sign you in
Profile: first name, username and optional profile pictureSo friends can find and recognise you
Places: name, map location, type, status, rating, notes, date visited, country, optional GPX routeThe core of the app: saving and showing your places
Photos you add to places, and which ones you pin to your profileTo show them on your places and profile

Reports you make about other users or placesTo review and remove content that breaks our guidelines

We don’t collect your contacts, advertising identifiers, browsing history or payment details.

Location

Pinpoint only uses your device’s location when you tap a location button (for example “Use my location”), to centre the map or set where a place is. We don’t track your location in the background. The location stored is the location you save for each place.

When you choose a photo, the app reads its date and location (if it has them) on your device to suggest where and when you visited. Photos are resized and re-saved before upload, which removes this embedded information from the stored copy.

Who can see your content

  • Your name, username and profile picture can be found by any signed-in Pinpoint user, unless you’ve blocked them or they’ve blocked you.
  • Your places, photos, notes, ratings, routes and pinned photos are visible to you and to people who follow you. Any signed-in user can follow you. If you don’t want someone to see your places, block them.
  • Your emoji reactions are visible to the place’s owner and to anyone else who can see that place.
  • The number of places you’ve visited is shown next to your name in search results and follower lists.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • Follower and following counts are visible on your profile.

Services we use

We share data only with the providers needed to run the app. They process it on our behalf or to deliver the feature you’re using:

  • Supabase: hosts the database, photo storage and sign-in. All the account and content data above is stored here.
  • OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap: provide the map tiles. Your device requests map tiles for the area you’re viewing, which shares your IP address with them.
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation) and Photon (Komoot): turn map positions and search text into place and country names. The coordinates or search text are sent to them along with your IP address. They are not linked to your account.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the web version of Pinpoint, and the web version loads code libraries from unpkg and jsDelivr. These services receive your IP address when the page loads.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards they provide, such as UK adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses.

We don’t sell or rent your personal data, and we don’t share it for advertising.

Our legal basis

Under UK GDPR, we process your data because it’s necessary to provide the service you’ve signed up for (contract). We also have a legitimate interest in keeping Pinpoint safe, for example by acting on reports and enforcing blocks.

On your device

Pinpoint stores your sign-in session and any uploads that haven’t finished yet on your device, so you stay signed in and uploads can resume. Sign out or delete the app to remove them.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. Deleting a place or photo removes it straight away.

When you delete your account (Profile › Delete account), your account, profile, places, photos, reactions, follows and blocks are permanently deleted. Copies may remain in our hosting provider’s backups for a short period until those backups are overwritten.

Reports you’ve made are kept so we can finish reviewing them, but they’re no longer linked to you.

Your rights

You can see, edit and delete your data in the app at any time. You also have the right to:

  • request a copy of your data
  • ask us to correct or erase it
  • object to or restrict how we use it
  • ask for it in a portable format.

To do any of these, email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Pinpoint isn’t intended for anyone under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access is restricted so people can only see what you’ve chosen to share with followers. Photos are kept in private storage and shown through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
