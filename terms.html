Community guidelines

Pinpoint is for sharing places you love with friends. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Don’t post or share anything that:

  • is sexually explicit, or sexualises anyone under 18 in any way
  • harasses, bullies, threatens or targets someone
  • promotes hatred based on race, ethnicity, religion, disability, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or similar characteristics
  • shows or encourages violence, self-harm or dangerous illegal activity
  • shares someone else’s private information, such as their home address, without their permission
  • is spam, a scam, or impersonates another person
  • you don’t have the right to share, such as other people’s photos.

Reporting and blocking
